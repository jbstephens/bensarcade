Cozy Hot Cocoa ☕

A real recipe from Dusty's Cozy Corner. Makes 1 mug.

You need

  • 1 cup of milk
  • 1 tablespoon of cocoa powder (unsweetened)
  • 1 tablespoon of sugar
  • 1 tiny splash of vanilla (about 1/4 teaspoon)
  • A handful of mini marshmallows
  • A candy cane for stirring (if you want!)

Steps

  1. GROWN-UP STEP: Warm the milk in a small pot on low heat (or in the microwave for about 1 minute) until it is warm, not boiling.
  2. Put the cocoa powder and sugar in the mug. Add a little of the warm milk and stir to make a smooth paste. (A grown-up pours the milk.)
  3. Pour in the rest of the milk and stir, stir, stir!
  4. Add the tiny splash of vanilla and stir again.
  5. Wait until it is warm, not hot. A grown-up checks it first.
  6. Drop in the mini marshmallows. Stir with a candy cane if you have one.

Snuggle under a blanket, pick a cozy movie, and sip slowly. Goodnight, Ben!
